# Homepage Surface Brief — Lensworld

## Objective

Redesign the CoWin Glasses homepage around the approved Lensworld visual world. Produce three structurally distinct high-fidelity compositions before implementation. The visitor must feel fashion desire first while seeing a real entry product, verified price and direct shopping path immediately.

## Primary Product Story

Lead with G200 Sport Audio Glasses: USD 39.99, blue wraparound sport frame, open-ear Bluetooth audio, calls, sun lenses, 5–6 hour rated music playback and 43 g. Do not assign camera, translation or voice-assistant functions to G200.

## Required Opening Content

- CoWin Glasses identity.
- “Smart eyewear for music, moments, and movement.”
- G200 product name and USD 39.99 price.
- Direct Shop G200 action plus Shop All path.
- Language, currency, support/search/cart access.
- A visible transition into the broader catalogue: GL1, V03-T5 and/or GL6 may appear using only verified fixture content.

## Fixed Visual World

The glasses are the interface. Use an enormous frame, optical masks, blue mirrored lens depth, matte black material, daylight white space and one electric-chartreuse action color. Avoid conventional hero cards, floating glass panels, generic dashboard grids and imitation competitor layouts.

## Composition Variants to Explore

1. Dual-lens gateway: two distinct lifestyle contexts contained inside the left and right lens, with commerce embedded in the optical geometry.
2. Infinite visor rail: a wide shield-lens world behaving like a cinematic product reel, with controls anchored to hardware details.
3. Lens tunnel: layered frames create a scroll narrative from movement to everyday to capture, with the model sequence visible from the opening.

## Responsive Rule

Desktop may use both lenses simultaneously. Mobile must become a deliberate one-lens-at-a-time chapter system with a sticky bridge rail for model, price and CTA. RTL must mirror content order and directional affordances.

## Acceptance Gate

Do not change production components until the user approves one of the three compositions. Store every comp and its exact generation prompt under `.impeccable/mocks/`, with approval state in a sidecar.
