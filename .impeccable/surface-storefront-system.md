# COWIN Lensworld storefront system

## Visual thesis

The storefront behaves like an optical instrument, not a conventional ecommerce template. Black structure, mirror-blue optical fields, off-white editorial space, and chartreuse action signals form the shared visual language. Pages should feel related without repeating the homepage composition verbatim.

## Page families

- **Discover** — Shop, collections, search, and compare use oversized route typography, lens-shaped product fields, dense specification rails, and direct actions.
- **Decide** — Product details use a split optical stage with media on the left and a dark configuration console on the right. Supporting detail follows as numbered editorial bands.
- **Operate** — Cart, checkout, and account use strong hierarchy, numbered task stages, restrained form surfaces, and high-contrast summaries.
- **Learn** — Guides, app, support, policies, news, and blog use a field-guide rhythm: index rail, editorial copy, precise dividers, and occasional optical image fields.

## Guardrails

- Preserve the approved homepage and extend its system instead of cloning its hero.
- Keep only one primary chartreuse action in each viewport.
- Prefer lines, rails, and hard spatial transitions over generic rounded cards.
- Product imagery may sit inside lens silhouettes; informational content must remain rectangular and highly readable.
- RTL changes reading order and alignment while preserving the same hierarchy.
- All interactions retain visible focus states and reduced-motion support.

## Responsive behavior

Desktop layouts use asymmetric two-column stages and sticky summaries. Tablet collapses dense comparison areas into horizontal scroll regions. Mobile becomes a single editorial spine with full-width actions, swipeable product rails, compact headers, and no clipped metadata.
