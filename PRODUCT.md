# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are travelers, content creators, cyclists and outdoor users, plus people who value everyday calls, music and translation. They evaluate the glasses on a phone first and expect the desktop experience to feel premium.

## Product Purpose

CoWin Glasses is a multilingual direct-to-consumer storefront for smart eyewear. It helps visitors understand which frame fits music and movement, everyday use, travel, capture, translation or prescription needs, then confidently choose a model and place an order.

## Positioning

CoWin brings open-ear audio, phone-connected smart functions and wearable frame styles into approachable eyewear for music, moments and movement, with product capabilities represented per actual model rather than claimed universally.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare frame shape, lens type, color, feature set and USD price; browse on mobile; add products to a persistent cart; calculate destination shipping; create a member account during checkout; and later review orders across browsers. Glasses pair to compatible iOS or Android phones over Bluetooth. Translation-capable models require the phone app and phone internet connection.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six URL locales: English, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Korean; Arabic is RTL.
- Language and currency are independent. Local prices are estimates; final payment is charged in USD.
- Confirmed published catalogue currently includes G200, G06-L, G06-T, G300, GL1, GL6, GL7, GL8, GL12-8, GL12-9, GL15-1, GL15-2, GL16-3, V03 Pro, V03-T5, V03-T6 and V03-T8.
- G200 is the strongest confirmed entry product: USD 39.99 launch price, wraparound sport frame, open-ear Bluetooth audio, calls and sun lens, with 5–6 hour rated music playback and 43 g weight.
- Other published model names, supplied imagery, prices and transcribed specification rows are usable; unconfirmed feature assignments must not be invented.
- Product color is a SKU choice but does not change price or dispatch rules.
- Global shipping excludes Brazil. Dispatch target is within three business days; 30-day returns and six-month limited warranty apply.
- Checkout supports card handoff through Oceanpayment and bank-transfer request; browser return pages alone never mark an order paid.
- The redesign must preserve routing, localization, accessibility, responsive behavior, cart and checkout behavior. This concept phase changes no production code.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: CoWin Glasses.
- Main proposition: “Smart eyewear for music, moments, and movement.”
- Personality: young, fashion-forward, technological, sporty and creator-oriented; premium without feeling old-fashioned.
- The homepage should balance brand desire with sales clarity: fashion-led first impression, while core products, real prices and purchase paths remain visible in the first viewport.
- Avoid copying the specific visual design of Ray-Ban, Meta or Chamelo.
- Existing homepage visuals are not required to remain.

## Product Principles

1. Product truth outranks dramatic marketing claims.
2. Make the difference between sport audio, everyday frames and higher-capability models immediately understandable.
3. Let a visitor reach a real product and price without sacrificing brand desire.
4. Design mobile-first and preserve complete multilingual and RTL behavior.
5. Treat generated imagery as art direction until approved production assets replace it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, keyboard-visible controls, sufficient contrast, descriptive imagery, reduced-motion support, responsive text and layouts, and mirrored navigation/interaction direction for Arabic.
